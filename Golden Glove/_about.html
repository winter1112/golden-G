About GoldenGlove

A passion for creativity, fashion and jewellery design

Golden Glove was created from a passion for creativity, fashion and jewellery design. The business focuses on creating jewellery that allows customers to express their individual style with timeless pieces that feel personal and premium.

About the Designer

The designer creates jewellery by first developing original sketches and then turning those ideas into finished pieces. Different materials, shapes and styles are explored to create unique designs.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create beautiful and affordable jewellery while providing customers with a personalised shopping experience and a memorable connection to each piece.
